PRIVACY POLICY

ONE BUMPER

Effective date: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MAY WORKS handles information in ONE BUMPER.

1. Information stored by the app

ONE BUMPER does not require an account or sign-in. Gameplay information such as best score, tutorial state, sound and haptic preferences, attempt counts, and gameplay statistics may be stored locally on your device. This local information is used to provide game functionality and is not intentionally transmitted by MAY WORKS to its own server.

2. Advertising and third-party services

ONE BUMPER uses Google AdMob to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process information in connection with advertising, consent, fraud prevention, measurement, and related services. The information processed by Google may vary depending on your device, region, consent choices, and system settings.

For details about Google's handling of data, see Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, ONE BUMPER may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before allowing tracking that requires this permission. You may allow or deny the request. Your choice can later be changed in iOS Settings, subject to Apple's system controls.

4. Consent choices

Where required, the app may use Google's User Messaging Platform to request advertising-related consent. Advertising behavior may differ based on your consent status and applicable law.

5. Notifications

ONE BUMPER does not currently request notification permission for normal gameplay.

6. Children's privacy

ONE BUMPER is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a MAY WORKS account or server. Third-party services used by the app are governed by their own policies and applicable platform requirements.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data generally remains on the device until it is removed by the operating system, cleared, or the app is deleted. Data processed by third-party advertising services is subject to the retention practices of those providers.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to mayworks.support@gmail.com.
