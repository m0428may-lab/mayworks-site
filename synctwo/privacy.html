PRIVACY POLICY

SyncTwo

Effective date: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MAY WORKS handles information in SyncTwo.

1. Information stored by the app

SyncTwo does not require an account or sign-in. Game progress and settings, including items such as selected skins and daily challenge completion, may be stored locally on your device. This local information is used to provide app functionality and is not intentionally transmitted by MAY WORKS to its own server.

2. Advertising and third-party services

SyncTwo uses Google AdMob to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process information in connection with advertising, consent, fraud prevention, measurement, and related services. The information processed by Google may vary depending on your device, region, consent choices, and system settings.

For details about Google's handling of data, see Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, SyncTwo may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before allowing tracking that requires this permission. You may allow or deny the request. Your choice can later be changed in iOS Settings, subject to Apple's system controls.

4. Data storage

Locally stored game data generally remains on the device until it is removed by the operating system, cleared, or the app is deleted. Data processed by third-party advertising services is subject to the retention practices of those providers.

5. Children's privacy

SyncTwo is not directed at children under 13. MAY WORKS does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a SyncTwo account or MAY WORKS server.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to mayworks.support@gmail.com.
